Biography & Research Focus

Decision architectures under incomplete models
and asymmetric tail risk.

François is an applied mathematician and entrepreneur. His mathematical training is in optimisation — convex, robust, and spectral — educated at ENSTA Paris, with a PhD funded by the DGA (French Defence Procurement Agency) and a postdoctoral fellowship at NYU's Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences, before being recruited as a researcher at INRIA.

Over three decades, he has designed quantitative and analytical engines for institutions operating under genuine structural uncertainty: global asset managers, sovereign institutions, and strategic operators navigating complex geoeconomic environments. He founded Raise Partner, an institutional portfolio risk-management technology company, and later co-founded Suzugia, a quantitative and NLP-driven hedge fund acquired by AllianceBernstein.

What connects these engagements is a single preoccupation: decision-making when the model is incomplete, cross-asset correlations shift endogenously, and the cost of drawdown is asymmetric. Within Merlin Intelligence, this lineage shapes both our Equilibrium Portfolio Series — using walk-forward convex optimization and Euler risk decomposition to read macroeconomic regimes — and eigenmind, the open-source spectral graph engine he originated to detect knowledge singularities across strategic corpora.

He is also CEO of praXvalue and leads the "AI Do It Yourself" practice at Orson.ai, extending the conviction at the core of Merlin Intelligence: organisations compound durable advantage when they build, audit, and own their analytical infrastructure rather than rent black-box consensus.

A system designed for uncertainty does not eliminate it. It makes the cost of being wrong survivable — and the advantage of being early computable.
