When a Portfolio Becomes a Market Barometer

Introduces the quantitative architecture and philosophical mandate behind our cross-asset equilibrium barometer. Across an eight-year out-of-sample walk-forward evaluation (2018–2026) over twelve liquid ETFs, a non-GenAI meta-allocator selecting weekly among 60 candidate agents compounds capital while bounding maximum drawdowns (-6.5% 8Y MaxDD, 4.22 3Y Calmar)—turning weekly simplex weights and Euler risk shares into an objective gauge of the macroeconomic regime.

Tightening Bounds, Compressing Risk: The Portfolio's Shift to Liquidity, and the Macro Fractures Behind the Move

Analyzes the portfolio transition observed since the beginning of September across seven weekly reviews. In late September, the walk-forward learning process activated a markedly more cautious agent that tightens concentration limits and lowers its return hurdle—stepping back into risk-free cash (SHV at 31.1%), eliminating long duration (TLT 0%) and gold (GLD 0%), exiting China equities (MCHI 0.07%), and compressing annualized portfolio volatility to 2.67%. Guided by our OWL/SKOS domain ontology, we map these shifts to four structural macro fractures.
